About MOIC-LN

Zambia's Trusted Infrastructure Partner

MOIC-LN Consortium Limited is a Zambian infrastructure and engineering company specializing in the development, financing, operation and maintenance of modern toll road infrastructure. We deliver turnkey solutions that meet international standards while driving economic growth across Zambia.

Guided by our core values of Speed · Safety · Service, we combine technical expertise, innovation and unwavering commitment to quality in every project we undertake.
